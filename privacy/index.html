01

Overview

Prabhaya (“the app”, “we”) is a free digital-awareness app built for Telugu-speaking communities, with a focus on Telangana. It helps you find government schemes, vaccination and nutrition guidance, period care, finance awareness and nearby services.

Our promise in plain language: collect as little as possible, process on your device when we can, and be explicit about the few times data leaves your phone. This policy is available in English, Telugu and Hindi; if the versions ever differ, the English version prevails.

02

Nothing is collected silently. Here is every category of data the app touches, and exactly where it goes.

03

How we use information

  • Answer your questions and match you to government schemes you may be eligible for.
  • Show nearby facilities and open maps when you ask for directions.
  • Read answers aloud in your chosen language.
  • Remember your language, tracker entries and settings between visits.
  • If you opt in to diagnostics, understand which features help most and fix what fails.

We do not sell data, show ads, or build marketing profiles. Ever.

04

Permissions

The app asks for three permissions. Each is tied to a feature you choose to use, and each can be denied.

Microphone

Used only while you hold a voice session to ask questions aloud. Deny it and typing works as normal.

Location

Used only for Nearby help and health directions. Deny it and you can enter your location manually.

Internet & network state

Downloads optional translation models, reaches cloud answers when enabled, and checks connectivity. Core scheme matching works fully offline.

Change any permission anytime: Android Settings → Apps → Prabhaya → Permissions.
05

Third-party services

Some features rely on trusted providers. When they do, your data is handled under that provider's own policy:

OpenAI Cloud chat answers and question embeddings, only when cloud mode is on. Your question and a short window of recent chat are sent over HTTPS. Policy
Google ML Kit On-device Telugu ↔ English translation. Models download once; translation then runs offline on your phone. Policy
Android Speech & TTS Voice input and read-aloud answers through the Android system's speech services. Policy
Google Play services Device location for nearby search, processed on your device. Policy
Google Maps Opened only when you tap a directions link; your chosen location is passed to Maps. Policy

Your health entries and app preferences are never sent to any of these services.

06

Diagnostics, in detail

If — and only if — you opt in during onboarding or later in Profile, the app sends pseudonymous usage events to our research server so we can improve answer quality.

An event contains: a random installation ID (not your name or number), a session ID, the event type, your language, app version, Android version, and — for messages — a one-way SHA-256 hash plus character count. The message text itself never leaves your phone.

Journey events are kept for about 18 months and then purged; anonymous aggregate counts may be kept longer. Turning diagnostics off in Profile stops collection immediately and deletes the unsent queue on your phone. To delete data already on the server, contact us below.

07

Storage & security

Tracker entries, preferences and chat history live in your phone's private app storage. All network requests use HTTPS, keys you add are kept in the app's secure key store, and diagnostics travel under a bearer token. No system is perfect — so we deliberately keep very little that could ever be exposed.

08

Your rights & choices

You stay in control of every piece of data:

Access & delete local data

Everything on your phone is yours. Clear it via Settings → Apps → Prabhaya → Storage → Clear storage, or simply uninstall.

Withdraw consent

Turn diagnostics off in Profile anytime; the unsent local queue is purged on the spot.

Revoke permissions

Deny microphone or location without losing core features — typing and manual location always work.

Server-side deletion

Ask us to delete diagnostics tied to your installation. We confirm within 30 days.

Raise a concern

Think your data was misused? Contact us first — we will investigate and make it right.

These choices reflect the rights in India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

09

Children's privacy

Prabhaya is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. The effective date above always marks the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app. Continuing to use Prabhaya after an update means you accept the revised policy.
