What Prabhaya does

One app for the questions that matter most — no sign-up, no clutter.

Government schemes

Answer a few simple questions and find Telangana and central schemes you may be eligible for.

Vaccination tracker

Track your child's doses against the national schedule — records stay on your phone.

Period care

Simple, private cycle tracking with guidance. Entries never leave your device.

Nutrition guidance

Practical food and nutrition advice for mothers and children, in your language.

Nearby help

Find hospitals and services near you, with one-tap directions in Maps.

Money & safety awareness

Loans, savings and cyber-fraud awareness explained in plain, simple language.
